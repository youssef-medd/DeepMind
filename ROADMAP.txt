              Personal Life Operating System (Multi-Agent AI)

TECH STACK: TypeScript, LangGraph, LangChain Core
ARCHITECTURE: 3-Tier Multi-Agent (Orchestrator → Supervisors → Workers)

================================================================================
 WHAT YOU'RE BUILDING
================================================================================

A system where you ask a natural-language question like "How was my week?" and
specialized AI agents fan out across your health, productivity, and finance
data, find cross-domain patterns, and respond with insight + actions.
